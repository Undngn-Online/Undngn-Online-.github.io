      A Meeting at the Castle Museum

      Artha didn’t plan for anything special that day,
      just a quiet walk through a castle museum, surrounded by old stone walls and history.

      Then he saw Sandra, standing in front of a painting, lost in curiosity.
      A simple conversation turned into shared smiles and soft laughter.

      Among stories of the past,
      they found the beginning of something new.

      From History to Forever

      The castle museum was filled with old stories,
      but Artha and Sandra chose to create their own.

      From a brief meeting grew comfort.
      From simple talks came certainty.

      Today, they walk hand in hand,
      carrying that first memory into their future.

      Because their love was not a coincidence,
      but a destiny finding its way.
